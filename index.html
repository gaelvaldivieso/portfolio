AI maintenance platform for a printing and packaging plant

Gael AI Consulting · Jul 2026 – Present
Result

Replaced a vacant maintenance-coordinator role. Logging a maintenance request is >80% faster. Used every day by the plant manager and the technician.

Problem

Corrective maintenance at a ~550-employee printing and packaging plant (304 machines) ran on paper.

What I built

An installable web app (PWA) covering the whole workflow: request → diagnosis → quotation → approval → resolution. Role-based login, device revocation, role-targeted push notifications and self-updating deployments.

How it works
  • Voice dictation with automatic text clean-up.
  • Photo analysis that suggests each request's priority and classification.
  • Voice-dictated closure reports turned into structured data, with automatic time tracking and a formatted closure email.
  • Connected to the company's legacy FileMaker ERP: live 304-machine catalogue, two-way sync of requests, and an offline queue with automatic retry for unreliable plant connectivity.
Business side

Scoped, priced and sold a phased digitisation roadmap. Phase 1 approved by the client in Sep 2026.

In progress

Automated supplier-quote intake: inbound emails are verified, AI extracts amount, currency and terms, quotes are matched to the right machine and unmatched ones flagged for review.

Operations control tower for a national delivery network

FedEx, official representative in Ecuador (Grupo Entregas) · Jun – Jul 2026
Result

Eliminated ~4 hours of manual reporting a day. Management now has daily visibility of at-risk and delayed shipments across a 39-branch network. Used every day since launch.

Problem

An office employee consolidated the daily shipment report by hand.

What I built

First, 9 VBA tools that cut the report to a few clicks. Then a fully automatic control tower: the report arrives by email and is ingested automatically, validated before any write, de-duplicated and logged. It includes a KPI dashboard, an alert centre, risk-sorted shipment tracking with full per-shipment history, and 30-day trends by branch.

How it works
  • 5,199 real shipment records processed with zero errors in the first test with real data.
  • Branches detected and created automatically.
  • 11 users in 2 roles; each branch sees only its own data.
  • Plus a Power BI monitoring dashboard (Power Query, DAX, Row-Level Security).

FedEx, official representative in Ecuador (Grupo Entregas) · Jun – Jul 2026
